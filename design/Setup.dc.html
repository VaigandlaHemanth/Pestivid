THE WHOLE FIRST RUN
One screen: language, three fields, a refusal
Language was a screen of its own and the account was the next one, so somebody standing in a field crossed two page loads before the camera. It is one screen now. No email, no password with a capital letter and a symbol, no crop survey, no acreage — those get asked when they are needed and not before. The panel listing what we do not want is doing more work here than any of the fields.
