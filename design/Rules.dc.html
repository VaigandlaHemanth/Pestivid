Rule 1, drawn out — the three claim tiers

This is the honesty risk that outranks the classifier. The chain proves one thing: that a file with this hash existed by a certain time. It says nothing about where it was filmed, whose land it shows, or what is growing. Every fact shown to an investor, buyer or reviewer renders in exactly one of these, with fixed wording.

Rule 3, drawn out — when the model declines

Two instincts pull opposite ways here. “Abstain must be prominent, or the farmer sprays anyway.” And “low confidence should look less finished, or it reads as a diagnosis.” Both are right, because they are about different axes.

So: an abstention keeps equal priority — same slot, same width, same headline size, equal or greater height — and loses all swagger: no fill, no gradient, no check mark. It also carries more content than a confident answer, because the re-shoot instructions are the entire payload.

And no percentage. Not until per-class accuracy exists on real Indian field photos, which it does not. A number we cannot stand behind is worse than no number, and “95.5%” invites a farmer to act on a figure measured on a tidy 1500×1500 dataset.
