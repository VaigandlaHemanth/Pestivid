ONE SCREEN, THREE STEPS
The video, the amount, and the one dangerous choice
This was three URLs and three drawn headers for one form, which is why the module kept a draft in sessionStorage: it had to survive two page loads. It is one screen now, the step changes in place, the bar above fills, and nothing is lost if a signal drops. The order stands: the server refuses a request with no video, so the video is asked first rather than forgotten at the end; and the settlement choice is last and alone, because it is the one question on which a farmer can lose their land.
