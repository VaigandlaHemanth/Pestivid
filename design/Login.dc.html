Funding a season is not saving money
Crops fail. When one does there may be no profit to share, and a total loss can return less than you put in. There is no capital protection, no guaranteed return, and no projected return shown anywhere in this product, because the profit does not exist yet.
We are not a bank, not a broker, and not licensed to advise you. Only commit money you can afford to lose.
What we hold, and what we do not ask for
What we hold
Your name, an email address, and a record of every season you funded, including the ones that failed. That record does not get quietly tidied.
What we never ask for
For a PAN, an Aadhaar, or a bank account. Not until money actually has to move, and you will be asked then, on purpose.

Why these two differ so much
Phone and code, not email and password
An email address is a barrier for this user, and a password they must remember is a second one.
Language before anything else
Picking a language is the only choice on screen that unlocks every other one, so it goes first , not in a settings page behind a gear icon.
A shared-phone warning, on the way in
Phone sharing is normal here and the research on it is unambiguous. Money screens behind a shared handset need saying out loud, at the point it matters.
Nothing decorative
No photograph behind the fields, no blur, no gradient. This screen is read in sunlight, and every one of those costs contrast for nothing.
