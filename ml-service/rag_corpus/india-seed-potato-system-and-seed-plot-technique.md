## Summary

Seed is the most expensive input in potato and the main way virus enters a field.
India moves seed down a chain from ICAR-CPRI breeder seed to certified seed, and the
seed plot technique lets the plains grow clean seed in the low-aphid window.
Certified seed is scarce, so ask what class of seed you are being sold.

## Body

### Why seed is the whole problem

Seed potato is vegetatively propagated, so virus in the tuber becomes virus in the
next crop. ICAR-CPRI calls seed degeneration from virus disease "the most limiting
factor in potato seed production." Seed is also the biggest cost. ICAR-CPRI puts it at "about 50% of the
total cost of cultivation"; NAAS Strategy Paper 14 puts it at "over
one-third". Either way it is the largest single line item. Both facts point the same way: buy clean seed.

### The seed plot technique

Clean seed once came only from the hills, where cool weather kept aphids below the
critical level. Hill seed was dormant at harvest, so the plains planted late and the
crop met high aphid numbers and degenerated.

ICAR-CPRI surveys from 1952 found that across the North-Western and Central
Indo-Gangetic plains, aphids cross the critical limit of 20 per 100 compound leaves
only after late December, or the second week of January further east.

That gap is the whole idea. The principle, in ICAR-CPRI's words, is "growing of seed
potato crop using healthy seed during low aphid period from October to first week of
January coupled with integrated pest and disease management, roguing and dehaulming
the seed crop during the last week of December or second week of January before
aphids reach the critical level."

ICAR-CPRI's requirements and dates:

- The area needs low aphids, or an aphid-free period of at least 75 days after
  planting.
- Planting: 15 October in Punjab; 25 October in Haryana, Rajasthan and western UP;
  5 November in eastern UP, Bihar, West Bengal and Orissa.
- Haulm killing: end of December in Punjab, Haryana and western UP; 10 January in
  central UP and MP; by 15 January in eastern UP, Bihar, West Bengal and Orissa.
- Withhold irrigation 7 to 10 days before haulm killing. Cover exposed tubers with
  soil at once, and remove any regrowth.

### What certified seed actually means

**Nucleus and breeder seed — ICAR-CPRI.** CPRI produces virus-free mother stock,
indexes tubers by ELISA against PVX, PVS, PVM, PVA, PVY and PLRV, and multiplies
through four field generations with inspection and roguing at each stage. ICAR-CPRI reports "about 2600 tonnes of
breeder's seed annually"; NAAS Strategy Paper 14 says about 3,000 tonnes.
The figures differ by source and year. Roughly a quarter now starts
from tissue culture, which cuts field exposure and so cuts infection risk.

**Foundation Seed 1 and 2.** CPRI supplies breeder seed to State Departments of
Agriculture and Horticulture, the National Seeds Corporation and others, who
multiply it by the seed plot technique. NAAS notes the produce "is certified at each
stage by the State Seed Certification Agency."

**Certified Seed.** Registered growers multiply Foundation Stage II, again certified
by that agency. Beyond that, Certified Seed I and II are multiplied by seed growers,
and NAAS notes their quality "is monitored by the seed producers/farmers themselves".

So "certified" is a specific legal claim by a State Seed Certification Agency, not a
word a trader may apply to any good-looking tuber.

### The shortage

India needs roughly 5 million tonnes of seed potato a year; the chain yields about
0.5 million tonnes of certified seed. NAAS says plainly that no institutional
mechanism monitors seed potato quality, so degenerated produce is often sold as
seed, especially to small and marginal farmers.

### How to source clean seed

- Ask which class you are buying — breeder, foundation or certified — which State
  Seed Certification Agency certified it, and to see the tag.
- Ask the State Department of Agriculture or Horticulture, the National Seeds
  Corporation, or your Krishi Vigyan Kendra for registered seed growers.
- Never save seed from a crop that showed virus symptoms. The tubers carry it.
- Check tubers before planting and discard rotted ones.
- If you must reuse your own seed, follow the seed plot dates above and rogue hard.
