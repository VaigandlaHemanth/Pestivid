Where to look instead

The subject supplies the palette. Not a framework, and not a mood board: soil, leaf, and paper. This app is about a field, and about a record of that field that someone else will rely on. Earth and growth for identity; a receipt-book neutrality for everything a number sits on.

One rule keeps it honest: hue is identity, lightness is legibility. Sample a hue from a real crop photo, then push its lightness until the pair clears 7:1. A hue survives that; a pretty mid-tone does not.

Type, and the script problem

Six scripts have to work: Latin, Devanagari, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Kannada. That single fact eliminates most characterful typefaces, because almost none of them cover Indic scripts — and a missing glyph does not fail loudly, it falls back mid-sentence.

So the farmer surface uses one family with genuine Indic coverage, sized generously, with extra line-height for matras. The investor and marketing surfaces — English-dominant, desktop — can afford a display face with character. That is a deliberate split, not an inconsistency.

line-height  1.65 minimum for Indic
clip-safe line boxes — no fixed heights on text
subset per script, budgeted in KB
font-display: swap, with a real fallback stack

Worth saying plainly: I have not verified which families cover all six scripts at the weights we need. That is a check to run before committing, not something to assume from a specimen page.
