Two things to know
Nobody tested the produce
The pesticide column is what the farmer typed into a box. There is no lab result behind it, no residue figure, and no inspection. It is a declaration, and it is worth exactly what a declaration is worth. If you need a residue certificate for an export buyer, you still have to have the lot tested yourself.
Why there is no price per quintal
A lot is sold whole. The farmer lists a range they will accept, you offer inside it, and the figure recorded is the one they took. Dividing that by the weight would invent a rate neither of you agreed to.
