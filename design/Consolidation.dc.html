Out of the menu, into the header

messaging and userProfile are not tasks a farmer sets out to do — they are things that happen to them, or admin. They become a header drawer and a header menu. That alone takes the farmer’s menu from eight to six before any merging.

Deliberately not merged

Record and Leaf check both open a camera, which is the obvious argument for combining them. They stay apart: one produces evidence someone will fund, the other produces advice about a plant. Merging them invites a farmer to submit a disease close-up as land evidence — and, as it happens, a close-up is exactly the framing the classifier refuses.
