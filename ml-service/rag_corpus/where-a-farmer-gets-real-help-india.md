## Summary
When an answer says "ask an agronomist", this is how. One free phone call reaches an agriculture graduate in your own language, and can be escalated to a specialist. Your district Krishi Vigyan Kendra can look at the actual plant. Soil testing is free. All the numbers below were checked on 22 August 2026.

## Body

### The Kisan Call Centre: 1800-180-1551
This is the fastest route. The mKisan portal of the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare states: "A countrywide common eleven digit number 1800-180-1551 has been allotted for Kisan Call Centre." It is toll free from mobiles and landlines on all networks. TNAU also lists "1551 (from BSNL Landline)".

- Hours: "Calls are attended from 6.00 am to 10.00 pm on all seven days of the week."
- Language: "Replies to the farmers' queries are given in 22 local languages."
- Who answers: agents "known as Farm Tele Advisor(FTAs). who are graduates or above" in agriculture.
- If they cannot answer: "Queries which cannot be answered by Farm Tele Advisor(FTAs) are transferred to higher level experts (Call Conferencing Experts) in a call conferencing mode." Those experts are "Subject Matter Specialists (SMSs) of State Departments of Agriculture (SDAs), ICAR Institutes, KVKs and State Agricultural Universities (SAUs)."

So the call centre is also the door to a KVK scientist. Ask for the potato specialist.

### Your district Krishi Vigyan Kendra
A KVK is the farm science centre for your district. ICAR describes its mandate as "Technology Assessment and Demonstration for its Application and Capacity Development", aiming at "Farmer-centric growth in agriculture and allied sectors through application of appropriate technologies." A KVK can inspect a plant sample, run a field diagnosis, and tell you what is registered and practical in your district.

To find yours, use the Kisan Suvidha portal at https://kisansuvidha.gov.in - open the KVK section, then "Find KVK", and pick your State and District. Kisan Suvidha is run by the National Informatics Centre and also carries Package of Practices and Directory Services. ICAR runs a separate KVK directory at kvk.icar.gov.in; that address did not open from our network on 22 August 2026, so if it fails, use Kisan Suvidha or call 1800-180-1551.

### Your block or district agriculture officer
Every block or tehsil has an agriculture office. The PMFBY operational guidelines confirm this is an official reporting channel: a loss report "may also be forwarded through the concerned bank branch, local agriculture department Government/district officials". Use the same office for advisories, subsidy schemes and seed sources.

### Free soil testing: the Soil Health Card
Go to https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in - the official portal offers "Get Soil Health Card" and "Find Nearest Soil Testing Lab". A soil test is the only honest way to settle a suspected nutrient problem. Do this before buying fertiliser.

### Weather damage and crop insurance: 14447
For hail, waterlogging, cloudburst, landslide or lightning fire, the official PMFBY site says: "Raise Queries, tell us about your Grievances & Report loss of Crop. Call us at 14447". That is the Krishi Rakshak Portal and Helpline. The scheme covers "localized risks of hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst and natural fire due to lightening affecting isolated farms in the notified area."

The deadline is short. The guidelines require "Immediate intimation (within 72 hours) by the insured farmer", through the Crop Insurance App, the centralised toll-free number, your insurer's call centre, your bank branch, or district officials. Take dated photographs the same day.

### Checking that a pesticide is legal on potato
The Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine and Storage runs https://ppqs.gov.in for the Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee. It carries the "Major Uses of Pesticides" list and a "Farmer Corner" with safe use guidance. If a shopkeeper recommends a product, check it there before you buy.

### Have this ready before you call
Village and district. Crop stage and planting date. Variety. Photographs of the whole plant and the whole patch. What you have already sprayed, and on what date.
